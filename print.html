Built for a double-sided T-stand: print Both sides on one sheet (duplex, flip on long edge), or print the two pages and slide them back to back into the holder.

Heads up: no Google review link is set yet, so the review side would send people to a "how did we do" page with your phone number instead of the Google review box. Print the menu side only until the Google Business Profile is live, then reprint.
Before printing: in the print dialog set Margins: None and turn Background graphics ON, or the black style prints blank and the layout shifts. Scale must stay at 100%.
