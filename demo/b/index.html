About us

Good food brings people together.

AL BAIK Chicken N Pizza brings the flavours people love under one roof on Stockton Boulevard: crispy fried chicken and wings, freshly made pizzas, burgers, seekh kabab, wraps and salads. Everything on the menu is halal, and everything is made for sharing.
